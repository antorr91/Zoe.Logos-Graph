Data and research gaps

The atlas as open data: what is studied, where the gaps are, and the files behind every page. The data are updated on the 1st and 15th of every month.

What is studied, and where

Peer-reviewed papers on each research theme, for each zoological class. Dark cells are themes that are rarely studied in that class.

Papers per species divides by the number of atlas species in the class, so classes with few species (reptiles, cephalopods) can look well studied with only a handful of papers. Switch to total papers to see the raw counts.
