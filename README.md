# Zoe.Logos-Graph

**A comparative atlas of animal communication.**
273 species · 2,292 peer-reviewed papers · an interactive knowledge graph.

🔗 **Live site:** https://antorr91.github.io/Zoe.Logos-Graph/
📁 **Repository:** https://github.com/antorr91/Zoe.Logos-Graph

---

## What this is

Zoe.Logos-Graph links species to the vocalisations they produce, the contexts in which they occur, the communicative functions they serve, and the peer-reviewed studies that document them — presented as an explorable, comparable, citable network. The site is fully static: open `outputs/index.html` or serve the `outputs/` folder.

---

## Coverage

- **273 species** across 7 zoological classes (Mammalia 129, Aves 83, Amphibia 18, Insecta 16, Actinopterygii 11, Reptilia 9, Cephalopoda 3).
- **2,292 peer-reviewed papers**, each with a verified DOI and open-access flag. Only peer-reviewed journal articles are included; preprints are excluded.
- **16 research themes** (vocal learning, referential signalling, syntax, dialects, echolocation, infrasound, alarm, cooperation, deception, multimodal signalling, parent–offspring communication, individual recognition, turn-taking, cultural transmission, emotion, honest signalling).

---

## Features

- **Species Explorer** — search all 273 species; per-species vocalisations, contexts, functions, frequency ranges, vocal-learning status, and supporting papers.
- **Knowledge Graph** — interactive D3 graph grouped by communicative theme or communicative function, with a vocal-learning filter and Louvain community detection.
- **Literature view** — papers organised by research theme, with search, filtering, and pagination.
- **Species comparator** — side-by-side comparison across any of the 273 species.
